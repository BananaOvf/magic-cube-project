Magic Cube Simulator (v1.0.0)

Key Features:
  • Face and slice rotations (including wide moves and whole cube rotations)
  • Interactive camera control (mouse drag to rotate, wheel to zoom)
  • Standard scramble generation (no consecutive moves on the same axis)
  • Two built-in solvers: Kociemba (Two-Phase) and CFOP
  • Save/Load current state (position + move history)
  • Solve timer with Spacebar hold; auto-saving of results
  • View, delete, and export solves to Excel
  • Animation speed control (0.1x – 50.0x), compact history view, splash screen

Technologies: C#, .NET, WPF, MVVM (CommunityToolkit.Mvvm), ClosedXML, Kociemba Two-Phase Algorithm.
